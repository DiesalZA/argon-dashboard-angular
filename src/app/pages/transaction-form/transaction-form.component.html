Quick Guide

Transaction Types

  • Buy: Purchase shares - adds to holdings
  • Sell: Sell shares - reduces holdings
  • Dividend: Dividend payment - adds to cash

Tips

  • Enter the symbol in uppercase (e.g., AAPL)
  • Include all fees and commissions
  • Use the exact transaction date
  • Total is calculated automatically
Note: Your transaction will be added to the selected portfolio. Make sure you have sufficient cash balance for purchases.
